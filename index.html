    I am a psychotherapist offering integrative talk therapy to adults. I am a Registered Member of the British Association of Counsellors and Psychotherapists and have an MSc in Counselling and Positive Psychology.

    ​

    Individual Therapy 

    I have experience working with adults facing anxiety, grief, depression, and adjusting to challenges that come with change. I integrate individual's strengths and curiosities, which may incorporate creative arts, sports and nature.  I aim to provide a safe space for clients to explore and build a platform of support for the next step of their journey. I am currently accepting new clients Thursday evenings.

     

    Organisational Wellbeing

    My consultation services are rooted in research and previous experience in the public, private and charitable sectors to improve individual and collective wellbeing.
