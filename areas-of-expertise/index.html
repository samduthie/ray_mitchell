My approach

I integrate talk-therapy and psychological research to support the holistic wellbeing of each client. I aim to provide a safe and supportive environment to explore. My practice is dedicated to promoting personal and communal growth and helping clients navigate life's challenges with resilience and strength.

Educational Background

My educational background is in Counselling & Positive Psychology and I incorporate current, evidence based research in session. I ask clients if they would be interested in learning psycho-educational information that has proven helpful to others in similar situations prior to integrating information in a therapy session.
